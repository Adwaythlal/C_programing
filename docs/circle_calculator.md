## is C program is used to calculate the area, surface area, and volume of a sphere.

- The user enters the radius of the sphere.
- The program uses the value of π (PI = 3.14159).
- It calculates:
- Area = π × r²
- Surface Area = 4 × π × r²
- Volume = (4/3) × π × r³
- Finally, it displays all three results.
